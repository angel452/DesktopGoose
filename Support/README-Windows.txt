Desktop Goose for Windows
=========================

1. Keep this whole folder together, wherever you like (for example in Documents).
   DesktopGoose needs the other files and the Assets folder sitting next to it.
   If you are reading this from inside the zip, close it first: right-click the
   zip, choose "Extract All...", then click "Extract".

2. Double-click DesktopGoose.

   The first time, a blue window may say "Windows protected your PC".
   That is expected: the app is free and not signed with a paid certificate.
   It is not a virus. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only do this once.

3. That is it. The goose wanders your screen. It has no window: it lives next to
   the clock (click the ^ arrow if you do not see it). Click the goose icon for:

     Take a Break Now     the goose brings you a reminder right away
     Shoo the Memes       closes every meme and speech bubble
     Quit Desktop Goose   stops the goose

Want it on your desktop? Right-click DesktopGoose, choose "Show more options"
(Windows 11 only), then "Send to" > "Desktop (create shortcut)". After restarting
your computer, double-click that shortcut to bring the goose back.

Add your own memes (PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP) to Assets\Memes and honks (WAV) to
Assets\Sounds. The goose picks them up the next time it starts.
