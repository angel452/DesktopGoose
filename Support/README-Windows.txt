Desktop Goose for Windows
=========================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere you like, for example Documents\DesktopGoose.
   Keep everything together: DesktopGoose.exe needs the .dll files and the
   Assets folder sitting next to it.

2. Double-click DesktopGoose.exe.

   The first time, Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC".
   That is expected: the app is free and not signed with a paid certificate.
   It is not a virus. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only do this once.

3. That is it. The goose wanders your screen. It has no window: it lives in the
   system tray (the small icons next to the clock — click the ^ arrow if you do
   not see it). Click the goose icon for:

     Take a Break Now     the goose brings you a reminder right away
     Shoo the Memes       closes every meme and speech bubble
     Quit Desktop Goose   stops the goose

Add your own memes (PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP) to Assets\Memes and honks (WAV) to
Assets\Sounds. The goose picks them up the next time it starts.
